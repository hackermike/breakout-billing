Security

A login password is optional. This app runs only on this computer (other computers on your network can't open it). For real client data (HIPAA), we recommend turning the password on and turning on disk encryption (Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → FileVault) so files can't be read if the laptop is lost. The password only controls who can open the app — it does not encrypt your data, and turning it off never deletes anything.
